# Blocked or Constrained Sites

Starter categories for future, evidence-based surveying:

- Social platforms with login or rate-limit constraints
- News publishers with subscription access
- Video platforms with bot challenges
- Financial institutions with high-risk automation controls
- Public-sector sites with session or geographic constraints
- Forums with anti-abuse throttling

Add named sites only with a source and a clear explanation of the limitation. Do not publish instructions for evasion.
