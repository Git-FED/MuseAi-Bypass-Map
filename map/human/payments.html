About paid access gating

A static GitHub Pages or Cloudflare Pages site can display payment buttons, but it cannot securely unlock paid content by itself. For real gating, verify PayPal/Stripe events in a Cloudflare Worker webhook, store entitlement status server-side, and check a short-lived signed session before serving protected content.

This page is the payment UI layer; it does not claim a successful payment until the provider and a server-side verifier confirm it.
