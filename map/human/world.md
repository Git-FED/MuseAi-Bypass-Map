# World Model

The world grows in layers. A new public destination becomes a place; repeated places of the same kind form a territory; stable routes create corridors between territories.

## Growth rules

- A place needs a canonical URL and a provenance note.
- A territory needs at least two related public destinations.
- A corridor needs a documented route that does not evade access controls.
- Stale places fade visually but remain in the log until reviewed.

The world is intentionally abstract: it helps people see the shape of the content layer without pretending that the web is static or that every route is always available.
